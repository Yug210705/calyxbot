# Investor Demo (3 Minutes)

## Objective
Demonstrate that Calyx is an enterprise-ready SaaS platform, not just a weekend AI wrapper project. Highlight security, multi-tenancy, and organizational capabilities.

## Flow

### 0:00 - 0:30: The Hook
- Open the Calyx homepage.
- **Narrative:** "Enterprise AI fails today because it lacks organizational context and security. Calyx solves this."
- Click "Get Started" and sign up as a new user (`founder@acmecorp.com`).

### 0:30 - 1:30: Multi-Tenancy & Enterprise Foundation
- Immediately upon signup, the user is prompted to create their **Organization** (Acme Corp).
- **Action:** Create the organization.
- **Narrative:** "Calyx is built from day one as a multi-tenant B2B platform. Data is siloed perfectly. Now, let's invite the team."
- Navigate to **Members** and invite `engineer@acmecorp.com` as an `Employee`.

### 1:30 - 2:30: Security & RBAC
- **Action:** Open an incognito window. Check the simulated email to accept the invite.
- **Action:** Log in as the new `Employee`.
- **Narrative:** "Because of our strict Role-Based Access Control, this employee cannot see billing or organization settings. They can only access the knowledge base."
- **Action:** Try to navigate to Settings. Show the "Access Denied" state.

### 2:30 - 3:00: The Future (Sprint 3 Teaser)
- **Narrative:** "Our enterprise foundation is complete. In the next sprint, we connect this secure organization to Google Drive, Slack, and Notion to ingest the company's brain, knowing perfectly well who has access to what."
- Close the demo.
