# ADR-044: Backpressure and Queue Management

**Date**: 2026-07-02
**Status**: Accepted
**Domain**: Scaling

## Context
As the Calyx platform scales, we need explicit architecture to handle backpressure and queue management to maintain stability, scalability, and security for enterprise workloads.

## Decision
TaskQueue requires priority tiers (LOW, NORMAL, HIGH, CRITICAL) and robust retry policies (LINEAR, EXPONENTIAL, EXPONENTIAL_WITH_JITTER). The Fetcher handles provider rate-limits automatically to prevent cascading failures.
